Why not just Duolingo

Every app teaches you to say hello.

Almost none of them teach you the one thing that actually gets you into trouble: the local rule nobody explains until you've already broken it. Three real examples — the kind of thing this course teaches, not the kind we made up to sound clever.

🇮🇹

Italy

Bars are legally allowed to charge two different prices for the same coffee — one standing at the counter, one sitting at a table. The difference is often double.

→ how to ask before you sit
🇯🇵

Japan

Tipping isn't just unnecessary in Japan — trying to leave one can confuse or mildly offend. Good service is already built into the price.

→ what to say instead of a tip
🇹🇷

Türkiye

In an Istanbul taxi, the meter is required to go on the moment you get in — and drivers aren't allowed to pick and choose their passengers.
