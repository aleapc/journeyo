Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Journeyo ("we", "our", "the app") publishes short offline language courses for travelers. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the Journeyo app or website.

Short version

No account is required. Your lesson progress stays on your device. We don't sell or share your data with advertisers. We don't show ads.

What we collect

  • Lesson progress. Which lessons you've completed is stored locally on your device (browser storage / app storage). It is never sent to our servers, because we don't have a server that stores it — the app works fully offline.
  • Microphone (optional). If you use the "record my voice" practice feature, the recording is played back to you locally on your device and is never uploaded anywhere or stored after your session.
  • Purchases. If you unlock paid content through the app, the purchase itself is processed entirely by Google Play Billing (or Apple's equivalent on iOS). We receive confirmation that a purchase was made, but not your payment details — Google and Apple handle that directly under their own privacy policies.

What we don't do

  • We don't require you to create an account.
  • We don't run ads or ad-tracking SDKs.
  • We don't sell your data to anyone.
  • We don't track you across other apps or websites.

Third-party services

The app links out to Google Translate for optional offline translation setup, and to your device's default apps (maps, phone) for a few practical shortcuts. Those services have their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review.

Children

Journeyo is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
